# Combinatorial CRISPRa Perturb-seq: Genetic Interaction Mapping

The goal of this analysis is to characterize gain-of-function transcriptional phenotypes, identify nonadditive genetic interactions, and explore gene programs relevant to functional genomics and drug discovery.

## Analysis workflow

- **QC and single-cell analysis:** guide assignments, perturbation coverage, normalization, PCA, UMAP, and clustering.
- **Functional characterization:** on-target activation, transcriptional phenotype strength, signature similarity, differential expression, and Hallmark/GO pathway enrichment.
- **Combinatorial interactions:** compare observed dual-gene signatures against an additive single-gene model; rank interaction residuals and examine gene-specific effects.
- **Biological interpretation:** technical GEM-group consistency, cell-state and cell-cycle composition, and a mechanistic case study of a top-ranked pair.

## Key observations

The exploratory analysis highlights strong transcriptional effects and nonadditive responses among **CEBPA/CEBP-family combinations**, including changes in erythroid- and myeloid-associated programs.
